## 3. Honest verdict

- **ALL grounding_deep win is directionally consistent but NOT statistically clean at n=30.** ALL beats BASE on 13/30 images (win-rate 0.43, mean Δ +0.267, t-read 0.77). With |t| < 2, the per-image paired test treats this as a real-but-weak signal at screen scale, not a slam-dunk: roughly half the images are ties/losses, so the aggregate win rides on a minority of drift-recovery crops rather than a broad sweep.
- **Single-arm components do not isolate a grounding win.** +R_rep is essentially tied (mean Δ -0.017, win/loss 8/9, t-read -0.06) and +R_anc is slightly negative (mean Δ -0.217, win/loss 7/11, t-read -0.79). The deep-grounding gain only emerges when both rewards are combined in ALL.
- **Diversity (unique-token ratio) winner is +R_anc** (mean Δ +0.020, win-rate 0.67, t-read 1.80). Note this contradicts the prior expectation that +R_rep would drive diversity: on this proxy +R_rep is -0.015 and does not show an isolated convergence-fixing win.
- **R_anc-saturation caveat.** +R_anc's flat/negative grounding here is consistent with the documented training issue: R_anc had near-zero reward variance / saturated, so the anchor signal was weak in isolation and mainly useful as a regulariser inside ALL.
- **Scale caveat.** This is n=30 (DIV2K screen subset 0801-0830), a screen-scale read, NOT the n=100 confirmation run. Per-image variance is large (std Δ on deep grounding ~1.5-2.5 points), so t-reads near +-1 should be read as 'within noise' and the ALL verdict treated as a promising trend to confirm at n=100, not a settled result.
